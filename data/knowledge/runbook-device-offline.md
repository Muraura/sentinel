# Runbook: Camera or Door Controller Offline

## Known causes
Network drops, power cuts and scheduled maintenance (always announced with a CHG ticket). CAM-B-WAREHOUSE-01 is a
known flaky camera that drops and recovers several times a day; a replacement is on order.

## When to escalate
Escalate when a camera covering a restricted area goes offline shortly after an entry to that area, when several
devices drop at once, or when a device stays offline for more than 30 minutes without a maintenance ticket.
