# Data Protection Policy

## Personal data
Global face-ID profiles are biometric personal data. Bulk export of profiles is allowed only for approved
migration jobs run by the platform team, with a change ticket.

## Bulk export thresholds
More than 20 profile exports by one user within an hour is a bulk export and must be investigated. A bulk export
from an external IP address or from an account with a suspected compromise is a possible personal-data breach.

## Breach notification
A suspected personal-data breach must be escalated to the Data Protection Officer immediately. The regulator must
be notified within 72 hours of the organisation becoming aware of a confirmed breach.
